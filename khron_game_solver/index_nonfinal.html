How to Play
  • Place the letters in order from left to right. 🖐️ No skipping or shuffling of letters is allowed.
  • Once letters are placed, you cannot move them.
  • Only words that are 3, 4 or 5 letters long will score points.
  • Words score horizontally, vertically, diagonally, forwards and backwards. Longer words also score the smaller words inside of them. For example, THERE scores THE, HER, HERE, ERE (forwards), ERE (backwards).
  • Once a cell scores points with a word, it’s locked! 🔒 Those locked letters cannot be used for future words. Plan carefully. Don’t score too soon!
  • Click on Tutorial on top for more details.
Scoring
  • Basic Points:
    • 3 letters = 4 pts
    • 4 letters = 7 pts
    • 5 letters = 13 pts
  • Bonus Tiles:
    • Double Bonus (Light Blue) – Worth 2×
    • Triple Bonus (Green) – Worth 3×
  • Advanced Scoring:
    • 2 or more 5-letter words only: +75 pts
    • 2 or more 4-letter words only: +50 pts
    • 3 or more 3-letter words only: +25 pts
    • Words inside of words matter for advanced scoring. CHAT is counted as a 4-letter word (CHAT) and a 3-letter word (HAT).
    • Any tiles left over at the end: -4 points each. ⚠️
